# Cable protection checks

Select a rule set per circuit. The circuit editor shows each measured or entered value with its comparison threshold. The Planner report includes the result. Missing ratings remain **Incomplete** and keep the affected circuit from being applied to the simulation.

## NEC 2023 PV DC

The ordinary method for PV DC source/output circuits uses 1.25 times the parallel STC short-circuit current for the maximum circuit current. The unadjusted conductor ampacity and the minimum protective-device current use another factor of 1.25. The corrected ampacity must carry the maximum circuit current. A linked MPPT gets its STC current from the module and string design. When one cable runs to each string, sizing uses the highest string current. For an unlinked run, supply the STC current per conductor path.

Enter the insulation-table ampacity, installation correction factor, and terminal-limited ampacity separately. The tool also checks the entered equipment limit, maximum DC circuit voltage, device voltage rating and fault interruption rating. It applies the ordinary method from NEC 690.8/690.9 with voltage and interruption checks. The checks use the exact entered device size, with no next-standard-size, 100%-rated assembly or alternative maximum-current exception. [NFPA 70](https://www.nfpa.org/codes-and-standards/nfpa-70-standard-development/70) · [University of Maryland Extension](https://www.extension.umd.edu/resource/working-solar-wiring-and-fusing-eb-2023-0676)

## IEC 60364-4-43

The overload checks use Ib ≤ In ≤ Iz and I₂ ≤ 1.45 Iz. The short-circuit checks compare the device's breaking capacity to the available fault current and its let-through energy to (k × conductor area)² in the adiabatic range. Enter I₂, conductor k for the chosen insulation and material, and let-through energy from the device data at the relevant fault duty. The checks also compare the entered circuit and device voltage and AC/DC rating. [IEC 60364-4-43:2023](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/28432) · [Schneider Electric overload checks](https://www.electrical-installation.org/enwiki/Practical_values_for_a_protective_scheme) · [Schneider Electric short-circuit withstand](https://www.electrical-installation.org/enwiki/Verification_of_the_withstand_capabilities_of_cables_under_short-circuit_conditions)

## Radial AC fault duty and coordination

The fault study computes symmetrical RMS bolted current for a strong source at the device and a weak source at the remote end of one radial circuit. Enter resistance and reactance at the device for each selected fault type. A three-phase fault uses per-phase source impedance and phase voltage. Line–line and line–neutral or line–earth cases use the corresponding full source loop. The remote case adds outbound and return resistance at the entered temperature and the entered full-path reactance. Supply the neutral or PE size where applicable. The interrupting check uses the larger of the calculated near duty and any separately entered prospective duty. [Schneider Electric, earth-fault current](https://www.electrical-installation.org/enwiki/TN_system_-_Earth-fault_current_calculation)

For the device pair, enter the upstream **minimum trip or pre-arcing** curve and downstream **maximum total clearing** curve in absolute amperes and seconds. Enter points in increasing current order and use the manufacturer's published limits for comparison. The tool interpolates in log-log space and checks the time ratio at the endpoints and every curve breakpoint. It checks remote clearing against the entered time limit and, where the conductor factor k is supplied, the adiabatic limit up to five seconds. For the current-limiting region, a published selectivity limit for the exact pair and voltage can fill a gap in the curve coverage. Missing coverage is reported as incomplete. A documented curve conflict remains a failure. [Schneider Electric, device coordination](https://www.electrical-installation.org/enwiki/Coordination_between_circuit-breakers)

## Local rules

Colombia is the first local profile. Select the appropriate AC path from RETIE Libro 3 of Resolución 40117 de 2024. The tool checks 3% on an inverter-to-connection path under Article 3.17.21(d), 3% on a service at its disconnect under Title 26(f), and 5% on a feeder/branch path at design load under Title 26(f). The overhead service option also checks the stated minimum 8 AWG copper or 6 AWG aluminium, certification for outdoor service, and the AA-8000 grade for aluminium under Title 26(d). The project-authority option takes a documented limit and reference for other projects. [MinEnergía, RETIE Libro 3](https://www.minenergia.gov.co/documents/11566/4._Libro_3_-_Instalaciones.pdf) · [Current official RETIE register](https://minenergia.gov.co/es/misional/energia-electrica-2/reglamentos-tecnicos/reglamento-t%C3%A9cnico-de-instalaciones-el%C3%A9ctricas-retie/)

Enter the drop in the other portions of the path, or zero when the selected circuit is the whole path. The check uses the modeled resistive drop as a floor. Supply the reviewed segment drop when reactance or other installation conditions affect it. The scope is a radial AC pair and the stated RETIE checks. Asymmetric and making duty, meshed supplies, inverter-limited and DC faults, grounding design, and utility-specific exceptions need separate study.
