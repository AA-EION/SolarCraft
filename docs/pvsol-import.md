# Import a PV*SOL project

From **Open**, choose a `.pvprj` and a location for a new `.solarcraft` copy. The source archive is read locally. On Windows, choose a destination folder. SolarCraft uses the original basename and adds a suffix if the name is already taken. The CLI call `import_pvsol` takes `source`. Omit `file` to preview. To create a project, supply a new `.solarcraft` file. In MCP these paths are relative to the workspace.

The importer accepts the observed ZIP formats 15, 20, 23 and 24 with embedded `Project.xml`, `DB.json`, and a recognized three-channel WBV. It pins equipment revisions from the archive, imports module areas and their saved orientation, and reads every wiring block. Saved MPPT assignments determine active module counts. A genuinely unwired area remains available for editing and contributes zero generation.

- **Series and optimizers.** Series-connected segments share one current. Supported per-module buck optimizers use their saved voltage, current and power limits and efficiency curve. The desktop wiring editors display these groups and preserve them when applying other changes. Advanced groups can be edited through `set_stringing`. Enter verified DC cable ratings manually for these groups.
- **Active tracking.** Saved north–south and east–west designs use one moving axis. Vertical trackers rotate the saved tilt, and dual-axis trackers follow the sun within both rotation limits. SolarCraft evaluates orientation, incidence and diffuse optics every hour. The Design page can edit each array’s tracking mode, axis and travel, and the ground coverage ratio for backtracking. The single-axis tilt slopes down in its compass azimuth. Archived comparison data provide system-level energy. Per-array tracker angles remain unverified.
- **Cold string voltage.** An archived `Verschaltungsgrenzen` test at 1000 W/m² supplies the cell temperature and uses the module's published Voc coefficient. The module system voltage and inverter input limits are both checked. Hourly power simulation continues to use the electrical curve.
- **Site shading.** Embedded 2D horizon points are interpolated around compass north. The replay blocks direct and circumsolar light at the interpolated horizon and reduces isotropic light by the projected obstruction. An archived uniform percentage reduces both components. Applicable saved 3D shading takes precedence.
- **Thermal systems.** Supported combined space and water heating designs restore the saved tank geometry, heating schedule, hot-water weights, boiler and the resistance heater or air-source heat pump. When the archive provides effective floor area and design heat load, hourly demand incorporates solar gains on four façades and daytime internal gains. Otherwise, the importer estimates hourly demand from the saved annual input and outdoor temperature. Mixed tank losses depend on water temperature and insulation. SolarCraft preserves the stored dispatch mode. When a Standard-mode archive attributes at least 99% of saved heat-pump electricity to direct PV, the copy includes an editable **PV-surplus heat pump (sensitivity)** variant. Subhour control, boiler fuel consumption and thermal tariff migration remain open.
- **Saved and edited shading.** The importer checks each physical inverter, MPPT, string shape and module slot. Supported ordinary and cross-area series strings use per-module I–V curves with full-cell or half-cell bypass groups. Supported buck optimizers use the shaded module’s curve within the archived voltage, current, power and efficiency limits. The same solver accepts substring shadows from bound 3D fields or obstacle roofs after edits. For native scenes, assign an array’s routes in the Scene inspector or send a `bind_string_routes` scene edit with `array` and `bindings`. Each binding supplies `route` and `binding: {unit_id, input, group, copy}`, with zero-based indices. A nonempty assignment must cover the whole array. An empty list restores inference by route length and order. Generic scene exchange requires destination-project assignments.
- **Editable 3D scenes.** The observed DirectX mesh and drawn roofs restore editable objects, panels and routes. The importer supports rectangular shed, gable and hip shells, attached shed and gabled dormers, and upright trees. Two Dormers restores 36 modules across its two parallel roofs. Translations carry attached roof modules and preserve their string routes. Re-place panels after changing the supporting roof’s pitch or shape. The shells omit overhang surfaces, roof cutouts, openings, slab thickness and textures. Object-only scenes retain archived shade. With bound module placements a committed edit switches to SolarCraft’s geometry.
- **Rear irradiance.** Applicable saved hourly or monthly rear profiles are used by default. Without one, bound finite ground racks over a horizontal datum use scene rays for rear beam, sky visibility and single-bounce ground reflection. The model includes mutual row shadows, finite edges and visible scene obstructions, and applies the array-average rear irradiance to the module curves. When finite rack geometry is unavailable, saved ViewFactor pitch, width, clearance and mounting shade support the periodic-row estimate. The bifacial settings in Design or `force_geometry: true` under `/arrays/N/bifacial_rows` force an independent estimate. Results include monthly rear irradiation and the source.
- **PhotoPlan.** Supported four-line and rectangle records restore the original source photograph, calibration, a rectified view, the roof outline and saved module placements. The importer checks its projected outline against the archived image-space polygon before attaching the layout. Use the Roof editor to inspect or modify it. Other calibration types, multi-plane files and obstacle records still require migration.
- **Legacy island designs.** An identical set of battery clusters on one AC bus becomes an equivalent battery bank. SolarCraft imports the battery and converter ratings, the generator where selected, and available appliance load intervals. It reconstructs supported appliance and generated-load shapes on its reference calendar. Import warnings describe the assumptions for initial SOC, battery dispatch, generator switching and load shedding.
- **Grid designs.** SolarCraft imports a recognized single embedded load profile at its original interval, or the hourly base-load ledger when available. A feed-in limit relative to the DC power becomes the corresponding kW limit; an absolute limit is stored by PV*SOL in the same field in units of 100 kW (0.025 → 2.5 kW) and is imported as such. Review the import warnings for settings that require separate migration.
- **Grid-connected battery systems.** The saved system type determines whether storage is enabled. An embedded AC battery system uses its integrated converter, including when the saved DC flag conflicts with the catalog. A generator-side or DC-link (catalog coupling 3) battery with a matching inverter assignment uses that inverter's hybrid port. SolarCraft imports the embedded cells, converter limits, part-load efficiency and enabled timed charge or discharge windows with their power and SOC targets. It starts fully charged and evaluates the windows on the hour. A hybrid supplies reverse AC charging during hours when its inverter is idle. Published internal resistance is converted from the catalog battery block to a single cell using its series and parallel counts. Multiple systems and boost, float and equalisation stages still require migration.
- **Electric vehicles.** An EV system with a matching embedded equipment revision creates an active fleet. Its 168 availability slots repeat from the start of the reference year and support multiple departures per day. The saved distance is weekly and is divided across the departures. The phase-switch setting determines the minimum charger power, and the saved PV-surplus fraction determines the start threshold. The Fleet editor changes the schedule and charging settings. The imported source remains visible separately. The hourly replay starts each vehicle at the departure target and uses a constant converter efficiency. An older SolarCraft import with only source EV data can be reimported from its original archive.
- **Tariffs.** Supported saved demand schedules and monthly charges are used for billing. Feed-in tiers are evaluated against installed DC power. Exact embedded revisions are recorded where available. Net metering, potentially distinct currency or tax treatment, payout duration and multiple consumer tariffs still require review.
- **Cable plan.** The importer binds supported saved AC feeders, strings and DC mains to their inverter or tracker. The cable plan retains conductor material, dimension and count with operating points computed from the current equipment. Imported dimensions are available for review and editing. Hourly routed losses require a separately applied circuit with validated ampacity and design inputs.
- **Loss settings.** Albedo, soiling and manufacturer mismatch come from the PV areas. The saved spectral loss reduces direct, diffuse and reflected irradiance before module conversion and appears in the irradiation ledger. The import sets SolarCraft's LID to zero and sets DC wiring loss to zero when the saved lengths are all zero.
- **Module model.** When every imported module is flagged for standard part-load operation, the project uses the two-diode model with PV*SOL's preferred standard-parameter fit (`two_diode_pvsol`): cell topology from Voc / 0.6 V unless the declared cell count is within four, idealities 1 and 2, the third series resistance on a 0.1 mΩ grid that gives a valid recombination current, and a shunt resistance that rises at low irradiance. A half-cell module whose first topology fails is retried with half its declared cells in series. A module the fit still rejects falls back to the one-diode model, as in PV*SOL. Other modules keep SolarCraft's one-diode model.
- **Module temperature.** Legacy reduced Reindl imports use the saved mounting class, module mass and hourly pre-soiling irradiance to calculate each array’s temperature. The calculation carries the previous hourly mean forward. Explicit mounting or Faiman coefficient edits select the Faiman model. For current-model imports with one monthly temperature series in `SimResults.xml`, SolarCraft scales the Faiman coefficients to match the archived mean temperature rise above ambient. The import warning states the method.
- **Electrical losses.** Legacy arrays with no module-level shades use the saved fixed diode voltage loss before MPPT operation. PV*SOL catalog inverter curves apply a relative derating on either side of the nominal DC voltage.

The net AC channel uses PV inverter output for a design with a separate AC battery converter or no battery. For an imported DC hybrid that still uses hybrid coupling, it uses the shared inverter's delivered AC after standby and reverse flow for grid charging. The hybrid books its idle draw only while AC conversion is idle. When available, a separate gross AC channel compares positive inverter delivery before the grid feed-in limiter. Existing saved SolarCraft runs can be rerun to obtain the new meters, and reimporting the source archive restores the separate gross reference. The DC comparison uses inverter input after MPP adaptation for PV*SOL catalog equipment, with the adaptation shown as a loss before AC conversion. Older runs show their pre-adaptation basis.

Run the imported design and open **Results → Saved PV*SOL comparison**, or call `compare_pvsol` with the imported `file`. The saved reference and the SolarCraft run are separate. SolarCraft compares annual energy and whatever monthly or 8,760-row reference channels the file provides. Supported thermal channels include pump electricity, direct PV supply, pump and boiler heat and tank losses. Pump comparisons also count matched, missed and extra on-hours above a 1 W reporting threshold. When the saved production, base load and heat-pump series align, the reference quantifies their coincident PV surplus. The results show the signed kWh and percentage difference, mean absolute and root-mean-square hourly error, and whether the current run uses the imported input hash.

| Reference | Use |
| --- | --- |
| SimResults.xml | First project-total PV DC and inverter AC groups. `EB_WR5_PVEnergieAC` is signed net inverter energy. `EB_WR6_EingespeisteEnergie` captures positive inverter delivery. Annual and monthly energy are stored in watt-seconds. An empty model marker means no qualifying saved aggregate run. |
| core.results.3600 | Recognized hourly PV DC and inverter AC channels. The time column ends each consecutive local-standard-time interval. Signed AC includes standby in the archived series. |
| OecForeCast.json | The signed hourly PV generation ledger is the AC reference when the simulator's own hourly export is absent. Available hourly series supply missing annual and monthly totals. |
| WBV | Embedded temperature, global horizontal radiation and wind. Clear-day irradiance timing selects the hour mark or the interval midpoint while preserving the calendar slot. Ambiguous files use midpoint for tracking or thermal modes and hour mark for other imports. Saved diffuse measurements take precedence. For reduced Reindl, current models use the Bourges clear-sky denominator. Older and tracked archives with an economics generation ledger and no current simulation marker use the legacy extraterrestrial horizontal denominator. The importer also supports the hourly Hofmann base model with neutral climatology factors and uses Erbs for unsupported splits. |

The comparison covers PV generation and supported thermal channels. Open areas include multi-plane layouts, seasonal geometry shading, vendor-specific optimizer control, reflected light from sloped terrain and roofs, module-specific rear mismatch, other photo layouts and storage topologies, subhour EV and thermal dispatch, battery and EV arbitration, additional billing rules, and equipment protection. SolarCraft applies its normal electrical validation to imported designs. If validation blocks a run, the saved annual totals remain available in the reference.
